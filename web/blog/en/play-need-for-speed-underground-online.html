How to play Need for Speed Underground online in your browser

NFSU.cc runs the original PC game of 2003 inside a browser tab. There is nothing to install and no account to make: you open the page, press Play and you are in the garage.

Updated November 16, 2026

What you need

A browser with WebGPU, which is the graphics system the page draws with:

  • Chrome or Edge 113 or newer, on a computer or on Android 12 and up.
  • Safari 26 or newer on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad.
  • Firefox 141 or newer on Windows, or 145 and up on a Mac with Apple Silicon.

A keyboard is enough. A gamepad works as soon as you plug it in, and phones get buttons on the screen. The details are in the controls guide. The project is built and tested mostly on Chrome, so that is the safest pick if something looks wrong elsewhere.

Start in three steps

  1. Open nfsu.cc. On a computer the first visit shows the launcher: a menu with Play, Quality, Game, Volume and Controls.
  2. Press Play (or Enter). The game loads and you land on the profile screen.

Later visits go straight into the game. A small button in the corner opens a panel with the same options, and its "All settings" link takes you back to the launcher.

How big the first load is

Reaching the game's menu downloads about 32 MB; the game program itself is 11 MB compressed. Your browser keeps the files, so the second visit starts faster.

The optional remaster textures are a separate 1.1 GB download. They are off until you turn them on or pick the Ultra quality level.

Play or Career, Original or Remaster

Play opens the game's main menu. Career goes straight into Go Underground; its progress can be "All open" or "Normal", where events unlock one by one.

Version picks between Original 2003, the game as it looked, and Remaster, which adds effects such as bloom, edge smoothing and sharper car paint. Inside the game, F4 switches between the two.

Where your saves live

Profiles and progress are stored in your own browser, not on a server. That means they stay on this device and in this browser, and clearing the site's data erases them. In the launcher, Options has a Saves group with Save a backup, which downloads everything as one file, and Restore a backup, which loads it again, also on another computer.

If it does not start

  • "This browser has no WebGPU": update the browser, or use Chrome or Edge.
  • It runs slowly: set Quality to Low in the launcher (no effects, 30 fps, 1280 pixels wide). The performance guide explains each option.
  • No sound: browsers keep a page silent until your first click or key press. Check the Volume row in the launcher too.
