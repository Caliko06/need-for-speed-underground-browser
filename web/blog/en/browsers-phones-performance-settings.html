Browsers, phones and performance settings

The game needs one thing from your device: a browser with WebGPU. After that, the launcher measures your machine and picks a quality level, and you can change every part of it.

Updated November 16, 2026

Supported browsers

  • Chrome and Edge 113 or newer, on a computer and on Android 12 and up.
  • Safari 26 or newer, on Mac, iPhone and iPad.
  • Firefox 141 or newer on Windows, 145 or newer on a Mac with Apple Silicon.

If the browser has no WebGPU the page says so instead of loading. Most of the testing happens on Chrome on a computer; other browsers and phones are newer ground, so expect rough edges there.

The four quality levels

On your first visit the launcher times how fast your machine draws its 3D menu and sets a level for you. You can change it in the Quality row.

  • Low: no remaster effects, 30 fps, picture 1280 pixels wide. For modest machines.
  • Medium: effects on, 1920 pixels wide, lighter filters.
  • High: everything on at the resolution of your screen.
  • Ultra: High plus the remaster textures (a 1.1 GB download) and MSAA 4x. It is never chosen for you.

Options that matter most for speed

  • Resolution: the widest picture the game draws. Fewer pixels is the biggest single gain.
  • Frame rate: 30 fps is half the work of 60.
  • Remaster effects: turns off bloom, smoothing and the other visual extras in one switch, without touching the gameplay improvements.
  • MSAA 4x: sharper edges, heavy on the graphics card. Leave it off on a laptop.

Picking Original 2003 as the version is the lightest way to play: it is the game as it shipped, with none of the extra effects.

Phones and tablets

A phone or tablet starts light on every visit: the Original version, effects off and 30 fps. The corner button in the game offers three profiles (Original 2003, Remaster, and Modest machine / iPad) plus the touch button opacity.

On an iPhone, add the page to the home screen to play full screen. The touch layout is described in the controls guide.

Changing settings later

After the first visit a computer goes straight into the game. The small button in the top corner opens quality, game, version, sound and keys; a change there restarts the game with the new setting. "All settings" opens the full launcher with every option, grouped as Game, Picture, Camera, Performance, Gameplay, and Sound and controls.

In the repo: web/gpu.js · web/audio.js · web/webgpu_gl.js
