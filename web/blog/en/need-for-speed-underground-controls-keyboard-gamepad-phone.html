Controls: keyboard, gamepad and phone

The page keeps the keys the PC game shipped with, adds a gamepad layout and draws buttons on touch screens. Everything except the sticks and triggers can be remapped.

Updated November 24, 2026

Keyboard

  • Steer← →
  • Gas↑
  • Brake / reverse↓
  • NitrousSpace
  • HandbrakeLeft Shift
  • Change cameraC
  • Reset carR
  • PauseEsc
  • Menus: acceptEnter
  • Menus: backBackspace

Three more keys belong to the page, not to the game:

  • Remap keys and buttonsK
  • Original / RemasterF4

Gamepad

Any pad the browser sees with the standard layout works (Xbox, PlayStation, most USB and Bluetooth pads). Names below are the Xbox ones.

  • SteerLeft stick
  • GasRT
  • Brake / reverseLT
  • NitrousA
  • HandbrakeB
  • Change cameraY
  • Reset carRB
  • PauseStart
  • Menus: moveD-pad or left stick
  • Menus: accept / backA B

The pad starts in menu mode and switches to driving when you pull a trigger, so in a race the first thing to do is press the gas.

Remap anything

Press K in the game, or Select on the pad, or open Controls in the launcher. Click an action and then press the key or button you want for it. If that key already did something else, the two swap. Sticks, d-pad and triggers stay fixed, and so do Esc and Enter. The choice is saved in your browser.

If you prefer smoother steering on the arrow keys, the launcher has a Keyboard steering option that makes them turn with the gamepad's steering model.

Phones and tablets

On a touch screen the page draws thin round buttons with the game's own words over the picture: GAS, BRAKE, NITROUS (which also holds the gas), two steering arrows, pause, CAMERA and RESET. HAND BRAKE appears in drift events, and SHIFT - / SHIFT + only in drag races or with a manual gearbox.

Android Chrome goes full screen on the first touch. An iPhone does not let a web page do that: tap Share, choose "Add to Home Screen" and open the game from that icon to fill the screen. What phones can and cannot run is in the browsers and phones guide.
