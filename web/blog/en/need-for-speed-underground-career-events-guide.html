Need for Speed Underground career guide: all 111 events, modes & bosses

Go Underground is the story career of Need for Speed Underground (2003). After the intro daydream race (Event #0), you climb through 111 main Underground events ending against Eddie (#112), followed by a final challenge against Melissa (#113) for 112 post-intro events in total.

All 6 race modes in Go Underground

Every event after Event #0 falls into one of six modes in EventDesc. Twelve of the Circuit events are multi-stage Tournaments (3 consecutive races scored by points).

Reputation, Style Points and visual rating

Winning races earns cash, while jumps, powerslides, drafting, near misses and clean laps earn Style Points (tracked at 0x758ad4 in the save profile). Higher visual car ratings (1 to 5 stars from body kits, spoilers, rims, paint, neon and vinyls) multiply your Style Points per race. Reaching Style Point thresholds unlocks magazine covers, sponsor vinyls and visual parts, and magazine events require a minimum star rating to enter.

Tips for clearing Hard difficulty

  • Upgrade performance before cosmetics on tight cash: save visual spending for star-rating gates, and install TJ's unique Junkman parts on the car you plan to keep for late events (see the complete 20-car list).
  • Use manual shifting only where it helps: Drag forces manual shifting; on Circuit and Sprint, automatic keeps you focused on traffic and shortcuts.
  • Back up your browser save: on NFSU.cc, career saves live in your browser. Use Options > Saves > Save a backup in the launcher to export your .ugd profile at any time.

Frequently asked questions

How many events are in Need for Speed Underground career mode?

Go Underground has 111 main career events (ending with Eddie at Event #112, since Event ID #85 is unused in the data table), preceded by the intro race (Event #0) and followed by the final challenge against Melissa (Event #113).

Who is the final boss of Need for Speed Underground?

Eddie is the final boss of the main 111-event Underground ladder (Event #112, Sprint), and Melissa is the secret final challenger immediately afterward in Event #113 (4-lap Circuit in a Nissan 350Z).

How do you get Unique (Junkman) upgrades?

Unique upgrades are rewarded by clearing specific timed challenges and boss races given by Samantha and TJ during Go Underground. Each unique part installs permanently on the car you are currently driving, so switch to your main endgame car before claiming late-game uniques.
