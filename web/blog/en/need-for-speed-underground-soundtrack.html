Need for Speed Underground soundtrack: all 26 EA TRAX songs

The original 2003 PC soundtrack contains 26 licensed EA TRAX songs: 19 rock, metal and electronic tracks assigned to races (IG) and 7 hip-hop tracks assigned to menus (FE), starting with Lil Jon & the Eastside Boyz's "Get Low". NFSU.cc streams all 26 original songs in your browser.

Full soundtrack included in the browser

How EA TRAX splits menus and races

By default, Need for Speed Underground plays hip-hop tracks in the garage and menus (FE), led by Lil Jon & the Eastside Boyz ("Get Low"), T.I. ("24's") and three tracks recorded specifically for the game by Mystikal, Nate Dogg and Petey Pablo. Once a race starts, EA TRAX switches to the 19 rock, metal and breakbeat tracks (IG), including Overseer, The Crystal Method, Static-X, Rob Zombie and Story of the Year.

Frequently asked questions

Does NFSU.cc include "Get Low" and the full 2003 soundtrack?

Yes. All 26 original EA TRAX songs from the 2003 PC release are included and play through the browser's audio engine. Click or press any key on the page once so your browser unlocks audio playback.

Can I play menu songs during races?

Yes. Open Options > EA TRAX in the game menu to set any of the 26 tracks to Menu, Race, Everywhere or Off, or toggle sequential vs. random playback.

Which songs were Need for Speed Underground exclusives?

Three tracks in Speed.exe are tagged "Need for Speed Exclusive": Mystikal's "Smashing the Gas (Get Faster)", Nate Dogg's "Keep It Coming" and Petey Pablo's "Need For Speed".
