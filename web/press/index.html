One-paragraph summary (ready to quote)

NFSU.cc is a free, open-source browser port of the original 2003 PC release of Need for Speed: Underground. Rather than emulating a PC at runtime, the project statically recompiles all 13,766 x86 functions from Speed.exe into WebAssembly (7.04 MB Brotli) and translates DirectX 9 to WebGPU with automatic WebGL 2 fallback. It runs directly in desktop and mobile browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) with no installation, preserving the full Go Underground career (111 main events), all 20 tuner cars, all 26 original 2003 soundtrack tracks, 33 PC cheat codes, local browser saves, keyboard, gamepad, and mobile touch controls.
