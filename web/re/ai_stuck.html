Rivals: how often they get stuck, and what GetUnstuck really is

Rivals & Race AI 36 verified Speed.exe addresses Speed.exe (2003, x86)
Updated 2026-11-25 View raw Markdown (.md) Source on GitHub

Question: how often are rivals stuck or sideways, what triggers GetUnstuck (type 0xa, update 0x452070), how long does the recovery last, what does it do to the car, and is the trigger or the timer the cause. V = read in code (address) or measured in the logs; I = inference; U = doubt. Nothing was run.

Answer

  1. The game's stuck test is not "speed < 2 m/s". It is: the car has been within 0.4 s of a contact/keep

list entry and its speed is below 5.0 m/s, or it is more than 20 m off its route line, or two special modes (vehicle+0x24 == 3 at < 25 m/s, == 4 at < 20 m/s) with 0x44e9c0(...)==1. V

  1. Trigger cost is tiny: 0.4 s under 5 m/s is enough, and the returned priority is 500, the highest in the

race selector. A rival that is merely slowed behind traffic or another car burns the recovery. V

  1. The recovery is a reverse, not a reset: the action's own speed target (racer+0x1e4) is set to

-15.0 m/s when vehicle+0x24 == 3, else -6.0 m/s, and route-node speed is disabled (racer+0x1f0 = 0). V Duration from the ctor slot 0x462ae0 = 1.3 s default, 1.6 s (float 0x3fa66666 / 0x3fcccccd) under one condition. Cooldown 6.0 s since the last creation (racer+0x408, 0x462aa0). V

  1. Measured: rivals spend 2.7 % to 8.8 % of their sampled seconds below 2 m/s (telemetry definition), with

single stops of 13.7 s to 17.9 s. GetUnstuck is entered 7 to 25 times per minute across 8 rivals (~1 to 3 per rival-minute), and 27 % to 38 % of those entries happen at speed >= 5 m/s, i.e. above the game's own threshold: those reversals are false positives, not recoveries. V (numbers below)

  1. So: yes, the trigger is the cause, and the timer makes it worse (0.4 s is far too short for a 5 m/s test on a

car that is 0.4 s behind traffic). A patch is drafted, opt-in, data only.

The trigger, line by line (asm, sub_451b00 = finder)

  • Gate: 0x435ca0 early-out (race not live), slot list head ctx+0x38c must be non-zero, 0x57a7d0 must pass. V
  • Off-route branch: cross product of (car - route point) with the route segment > 20.0 (0x6b7690) = off road

20 m, flag stuck. V for the constant and the compare at 0x451b87.

  • Contact/speed branch (loop over the list at ctx+0x3c, 0x451be0..0x451c76): for each entry it needs

(now - entry+8) * 0.00025 >= 0.4 (0x6b7680) and then own speed < 5.0 (0x6b7684), with a fuzz call 0x424b50(speed, 5.0, 0.001) that can still veto (I: a rare escape roll; U: exact semantics). On a hit the local flag is set to 1. V for constants and flow, I for what the list membership means.

  • Mode branch (0x451b94..0x451bdb): vehicle+0x24 == 3 and speed < 25.0 (0x6b7688) or == 4 and speed < 20.0

(0x6b768c), then 0x44e9c0(ctx+0x38c, 1) == 1 sets the flag. V for constants; U for what modes 3/4 are.

  • Return: flag set -> mov eax,0x1f4 = priority 500 at 0x451c9b, else -1. V
  • Two selector sites call it (0x44d924 in the race selector sub_44d8c5, 0x44dd89 in a second goal selector at

0x44dd45); both require current type != 0xa, current priority <= 500, and the 6.0 s cooldown vt+0x6c (0x462aa0), then set racer+0x408 = now and take the duration from vt+0x70 (0x462ae0). V

  • Lower levels also fire it: ctor 0x4519f0 is also called directly from 0x462656 (sub_4624d0, the "make action"

entry), so at least one path can create the action without the finder. I (callers V, semantics I).

The update, 0x452070 (what it does to the car)

  • racer+0x1f0 = 0 (speed no longer from route nodes), +0x1f1 = 1, +0x1f2 = 0, +0x1e0 = 1 (keep alive). V
  • Steer/lane target: 0x45c370(racer+0x20, ctx, 1.0, 2, 0, 25.0) (25.0 = 0x47c34f80) every tick. V
  • Then [vehicle+0x24] == 3 -> racer+0x1e4 = [0x6b7674] = -15.0, else [0x6b7678] = -6.0. V

The telemetry contract says +0x1e4 is the action's own speed target when +0x1f0 is 0, so the car drives backwards at 6 to 15 m/s, on a lane target, for 1.3 to 1.6 s. V for the stores, I for "reverse".

  • If ctx+0x38c is empty (no slot) it sets +0x1e0 = 0, the action dies, and 0x44bbd0 requests type 4

(Draft, priority 100, duration -1) at 0x44bc11. That is the sideways behaviour at low speed. V (addresses), I (link to the visible sideways drift).

  • No position reset, no teleport, no invulnerability: the recovery only reverses. V

Proposed patch (opt-in, data only, reason: trigger + timer)

tools/recomp/patches.py, both floats are read only by the three stuck finders (refs: 0x451c2e, 0x451eaf, 0x451ffb for the timer; 0x451c48/0x451c54, 0x451ec5, 0x45201b/0x452027 for the speed):

  • 0x6b7680 cdcccc3e -> 0000c03f 0.4 s -> 1.5 s (a car must be slow for 1.5 s, not 0.4 s)
  • 0x6b7684 0000a040 -> 00004040 5.0 m/s -> 3.0 m/s (18 km/h, not 11 km/h, counts as stopped)

Both behind RN_AI_STUCK=1, so original mode stays untouched and the log prints what changed. Not done here: the 20 m off-route branch and the mode 3/4 branch (needs to know what vehicle+0x24 is), and the -15 m/s strength (a 15 m/s reverse at speed is what looks clumsy; halving it is a separate experiment).

Not verified (U)

  • Meaning of ctx+0x3c list membership and of 0x44e9c0 (I read its call sites, not its body).
  • Whether 0x424b50(speed, 5.0, 0.001) is a random escape; if it is, the 0.4 s rule is per-tick fuzzed.
  • Real wall clock of w_a (frames per second is not 60 there); its percentages are shares, not rates.
