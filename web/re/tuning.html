Tool after each claim: G = Ghidra 12.1.4 (REA MCP or headless tools/ghidra/gpar.sh), H = Hopper export census/hopper/decomp.c, C = capstone tools/recomp/asm.py, D = data read with tools/re/carinfo.py. Answer

  • Where the numbers live. All car physics comes from one chunk, 0x34600 in game/Global/GLOBALB.BUN.

It holds 35 CarTypeInfo records of 0xc90 bytes (20 tuners, then 15 traffic cars). Each record has the stock CarPhysicsInfo (0x2b0 bytes at +0x110). After it come the per-car upgrade tables for levels 1..3 of every performance path, then one "Junkman" delta per path. V (G, H, D)

  • How upgrades reach physics. Every rebuild (RideInfo::RebuildPhysicsInfo 0x57f2b0) copies the stock block

into the ride (RideInfo+0x60), then applies each path's table row for the level in PhysicsUpgradeSpecification (RideInfo+8: int level[11], int junkman[11]). Some paths replace fields (gear set, tires, suspension, nitrous) and others add (engine/ECU/turbo torque, brakes, aero). Weight scales the mass. There is no formula per part: the designers' table row is the part. V (G, H)

  • Visual parts are a separate database: chunk 0x80034602, with 4,537 parts, 8,113 attributes and a string

table. Each part has a car hash, a slot/type byte, a level byte (0..4) and a list of (bStringHash, value) attributes (colour, texture, rim size...). The visual parts do not touch physics. The only link is the aero term in RebuildPhysicsInfo, which grows with the handling levels, not with the kit. V (G, D)

  • Prices and cash exist (corrected 2026-10-29 by night-opus-a2; the earlier "no prices" was wrong). Cash is

[0x76026c] (career+0x1374). The cost table is chunk 0x34a01 in FrontEnd/FrontB.lzc → [0x733f78+4*type], with cost L1/L2/L3 at +4/+8/+0xc (100..4000) and rating points at +0x10..+0x1c. Readers are 0x5a08f0 (visual) and 0x5a08b0 (perf); the payment is 0x5a2620/0x5a25a0. A tier unlocks when a career event whose reward is {type 0, price type, level} is won (0x5a1550). V (C, G, D). Full flow: docs/re/garage.md.

  • Two old labels were wrong. 0x583ea0 is not a speed cap. It is the generic lookup in a uniformly sampled

float table {n, xmin, xmax, 1/step, data*} with linear interpolation and clamping, V (G). The "tuning block" 0x705f48..0x705f84 is four of those tables for steering: maximum wheel angle vs speed (45° at 0 down to 2.9°/7° at the axis end 160; the unit of that axis, km/h?, is I: the exe gives only the number), a 6-point factor and a 0..1 return scale. They are read by the steering limiter 0x460a80/0x460de0, not by the garage. V (G, D)

1. Data: CarTypeInfo (chunk 0x34600)

Loader 0x57c8b0 (V, G+H): if the chunk id is 0x34600, it sets [0x734588] = (chunk+0x17) & ~0xf and, for each of 0x23 records whose +0xc54 == 0, calls 0x581d30 (it clamps per-car grip/load sums against limits at 0x6f0d30..). In the data, +0xc54 is 0 for the 20 tuners and 2 for the 15 traffic cars (D). Strings at the start of each record: name, name, CARS\<X>\GEOMETRY.BIN, .LZC, maker (D).

Upgrade tables, offsets from CarTypeInfo+0x110 (level n at base+(n-1)*size). Builder addresses are V (G, H). The field meaning is V where a consumer is named and I otherwise.

Values, GOLF (D): stock mass 1.33; weight L1..3 = ×0.949/0.900/0.850; transmission L3 adds a 6th gear (0.73); nitrous L1..3 = 2/4/6 s in 2/4/6 shots. All 20 tuners share the nitrous rows. Transmission L3 gives every tuner 6 gears. The ECU rpm rows differ per car.

2. RebuildPhysicsInfo 0x57f2b0 (V G+H)

edi = RideInfo: +0 car index (-1 = none), +8 spec, +0x60 output physics. Order of work:

  1. BuildCarPhysicsInfo 0x575960(stock = CarTypeInfo+0x110, out = ride+0x60, spec = ride+8): memcpy of 0xac dwords,

then suspension 0x575a10, tires 0x575a90, aero 0x575b30, brakes (inline), engine/turbo/ECU/nitrous 0x575bc0, transmission 0x576360, and weight 0x576250 last.

  1. Mode blend (only when [0x78a344]==0 && [0x7361a8]==0 and one of [0x735fa0], [0x78a345], [0x7361a4];

0x78a345/0x7361a4 are the drift flags per re_zones): 0x473fc0 picks one of 3 rows of the 0x6c-byte table 0x6f0fc0 from out+0x1c0 (thresholds 0.2/0.8 at 0x6cc960/0x6cc95c). The row pulls several fields back toward stock by factors and snaps out+0x1c0 to 0.175/0.5/0.825. V for the mechanics, I that this is the drift/drag handling assist. The map name "GetUpgradeLevel" for 0x473fc0 is wrong.

  1. Aero top-up: out+0x264..0x26c += (CarTypeInfo+0x7d0.. + +0x800..) * (level[0]+level[1]+level[7]) / 9 * blend

(0x6ccb88 = 0.1111): the L3 spoiler and ground-effects rows, scaled by weight+suspension+tires levels. V (G)

  1. 0x581b30(ride+0x410) builds the force-vs-speed table: for 75 speed steps it shifts gear when the gear's rpm

limit is passed and stores wheel force/mass. It then resamples the result into the table at ride+0x410. V mechanics (G); naming I.

  1. Tuners only (+0xc54==0): integrate (force(v) − drag k·v²/m) over the speed range of that table in steps of

1.3411 m/s (3 mph) until the drag wins. Compare with thresholds 0x6b5bd8 = {0, 280, 390, 500} and store the class 0..3 at ride+0x424 and the integral at ride+0x428. V (G, C); meaning I (performance rating shown in the garage / used by matchmaking).

Nitrous at race time (V, C+H): the CarState reset 0x464c80 sets [cs+0x3b0]=[cs+0x3b8] = ftol(seconds*4000+0.5) (4000 ticks/s, 0x6cc9fc) and the per-shot ticks [cs+0x3b4] = seconds/shots*4000 when shots ≥ 1. 0x465490 = available (time left ≥ threshold and not firing). 0x4654f0 = start: it stamps [cs+0x3bc] with the clock [0x73ad34], then subtracts one shot, or keeps the tank full when [0x7000e8] (infinite-NOS cheat or mode flag, I).

4. Unlocks (paint as the read example)

0x4d7eb0 (map: IsPaintLocked, V-graph) and 0x4d8180 (IsPaintOwned). V (G), with the arguments partly lost by Ghidra:

  • A paint part with level byte (part+0x11) ≤ 1 is never locked.
  • Otherwise the code asks 0x5a1550(&0x75eef8, key) five times, once per level 0..4, and the part is locked when

the answer for its own level is false. 0x75eef8 is the career/unlock state object (I).

  • [0x7677ac] bypasses the lock entirely. It is the "career beaten" flag: 0x5a0dc0 writes 1 when the last

event (0x71) is won, and the new-profile code 0x41cf40 clears it. V (G xref + patches.py:311 note). So beating the career unlocks every part. [0x735ec1] also bypasses (U).

  • "Owned" first looks for the level in the list at [0x737668] (count [0x73766c], 0x34-byte entries, type 1).

It then accepts when any of [0x7677ac], [0x735e12], [0x735ec1] is set. Otherwise it compares the level with 2 or 3, using the flags [0x735e10]/[0x735e11] and the same 0x5a1550 queries.

So the level byte in the part DB is the unlock tier: tier n opens when the career reaches it. The part record has no price field; the price comes from the per-type cost table (see garage.md §3). V (D)
